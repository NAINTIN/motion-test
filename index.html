How to get a decent reading
  • Hold still for a second after pressing Start. The app measures sensor bias then.
  • Move in a smooth, quick motion, then stop and hold still before pressing Stop.
  • Keep the phone from rotating much while moving.
  • Readings drift over time. Treat anything over a few seconds as a rough guess.
  • To test your fall, press Start and drop the phone onto a pillow. The free fall card gives a height.
