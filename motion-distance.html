How to get a decent reading
  • Hold still for a second after pressing Start while the sensor bias is measured.
  • Move smoothly, then hold still before pressing Stop.
  • Keep the phone from rotating much while moving.
  • Readings drift over time, so treat long measurements as rough estimates.
